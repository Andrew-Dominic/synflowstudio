Frequently asked
questions

Who are the engineers & designers?
You'll be working directly with the core experts at Synflow Studios. No juniors or outsourced talent.
How does onboarding work?
We'll invite you to a Trello board where you can share your design requests and track their progress in real time.
Who are the designers?
You'll be working directly with the founder, Brett. No juniors or outsourced talent.
Is there a limit to how many requests I can make?
No limit! Add as many as you want to your queue, and we'll work through them one by one.
How does the pause feature work?
Billing cycles are based on 31 day period. If you use the service for 21 days, and decide to pause, you'll have 10 days of service remaining to be used anytime in the future.
How do you handle larger requests?
We break them down into smaller manageable chunks.
What tech stack do you use?
Primarily React, Next.js, Webflow, Figma, and custom AI tooling setups.
What if I don't like the product?
We'll revise it until you do. Unlimited revisions included.
